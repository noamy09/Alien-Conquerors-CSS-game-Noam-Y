Remember your training: to align the spaceships horizonally use "justify-content:" and align them vertically use "align-items:". both of them accept as parameters: "start, flex-start, end, flex-end, and center". "justify-content" also accepts: sapce-between, space-around, and space-evenly, "while align-items" also accepts: "baseline and stretch". Finally, you may use flex-direction to choose at what order and layout are the spaceships placed. Examples for parameters are: "row, row-reverse, column, and column-reverse".
